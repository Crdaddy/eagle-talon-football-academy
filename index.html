WHO WE ARE

About Eagle Talon

Building Future Football Stars

Eagle Talon Football Academy is dedicated to identifying, nurturing and developing young football talent.

Through quality coaching, discipline, teamwork and competitive opportunities, we aim to help young players reach their full potential.

Our academy provides an environment where young players can develop their football skills while also learning important values such as discipline, respect and teamwork.
